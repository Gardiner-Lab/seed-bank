How to enter seeds, plants and lines

The seed bank can only trace where a seed came from if every step is recorded as it happens, the same way by everyone. This page explains the three kinds of record, then the steps for the usual routes: seeds from a stock centre, a transformation from T1 to a homozygous T3, and a cross.

The three records

🧬 Line GL-L-0001

A genetic identity: what the plants are. Col-0, myb21-1, the transgenic event 35S:GFP-4, the cross gfp-4 × myb21.

A line does not change from one generation to the next: the T2, T3 and T4 of 35S:GFP-4 are all the one line, 35S:GFP-4.

🫘 Seed lot GL-S-0001

A bag or tube of seeds: one line, one generation, from one source. Every physical bag has its own lot and its own label.

It says where the seeds came from (a plant here, or a stock centre), where they are kept, how much is left, and test results.

🌱 Plant GL-P-0001

One plant sown from a lot, numbered #1, #2… in that sowing. This is where you record what you see (phenotypes, photos, scans), and where the next generation starts.

They connect in a cycle, which is the lineage:

🫘 seed lot— Sow → 🌱 plants— Harvest one → 🫘 new seed lot, next generation— Sow → …

Because every seed lot remembers the plant it was harvested from, and every plant the lot it was sown from, any bag can be traced back to where it started, and forward to everything grown from it.

Golden rules

  1. Never make seeds by hand that came from a plant here. If the seeds came off a plant in the seed bank, open that plant and use 🫘 Harvest. ＋ Seed lot is only for seeds from outside: a stock centre, a colleague, an old box.
  2. Sow through the seed bank before the plants exist on the bench. Use 🌱 Sow on the lot you take seeds from, and print the plant labels. A plant that is not in the seed bank cannot be harvested, crossed or traced.
  3. One bag, one lot; one plant, one record. Harvest each plant you keep separately. Keep its code on its label.
  4. Do not make a new line for each generation. T2 and T3 belong to the same line as their T1 founder. Make a new line only for a new genetic identity: a new accession, a mutant, an independent transformant (🧬 Found a line), or a cross (made for you by ✕ Cross).
  5. Use the codes everywhere: on tubes, in the notebook (@), in emails. Every code starts with your lab's code (e.g. GL-), then L for a line, S for a seed lot or P for a plant, then its number: GL-S-0042. No two labs' codes are ever the same, and a code is never reused. The lab's lead can choose the lab code before the lab's first record (shown above the lists).
  6. Mark, don't delete. A plant that died: set its status to dead or discarded. A bag that is empty: tick used up. Records others came from cannot be deleted, and should not be.
  7. Use the same words for traits (pick from the suggestions: flowering time, leaf colour…), so phenotypes can be compared across plants and lines.

Seeds from outside (a stock centre, a colleague)

  1. Lines → ＋ New line. Name it as it is known (Col-0, SALK_012345); kind wild type or mutant; species; background (e.g. Col-0 for a SALK line). Check first that the line is not already there: search the Lines list.
  2. On the line, ＋ Seed lot. Fill in the Source (NASC N1092, from the Smith lab), the generation if known, the amount and Kept in.
  3. 🏷 Label: print a label for the bag.

Sowing, and labelling plants

  1. Open the lot you take seeds from, and 🌱 Sow: how many plants, when, where (tray, chamber). Each plant gets a code and the next number in that lot.
  2. Tick the plants in the list and 🏷 Labels for selected: print pot labels. A phone camera on the QR code opens the plant.
  3. If fewer plants come up than you sowed, set the others to dead (✎ Edit on the plant). Record germination on the lot if you counted it.
  4. Mark the plants you keep as ★ selected (✎ Edit), and the rest discarded when you throw them out.

A transformation, from dipping to a homozygous T3

Col-0 plants (dipped)→ T1 seed lot of 35S:GFP→ T1 plants: resistant ones found lines→ T2 lots: test 3:1→ T2 plants→ T3 lots: test homozygous
  1. The transgenic line. Lines → ＋ New line: name it after the construct (35S:GFP), kind population, background Col-0. Then Change plasmids…: add the plasmid from the Plasmid Editor, and Change parents…: Col-0 as background.
  2. The plants you dip. Sow Col-0 from its lot (step Sowing). These are your T0.
  3. Their seeds are T1 of the transgenic line. On each dipped plant (or the one you harvest), 🫘 Harvest, and set Seeds belong to to Another line: this plant was transformed, then choose 35S:GFP. The lot becomes T1 of 35S:GFP, harvested from that Col-0 plant: the lineage stays unbroken.
  4. Select T1s. Sow the T1 lot, on selection. Each resistant T1 plant is an independent insertion: open it and 🧬 Found a line. It becomes line 35S:GFP-4 (named after the plant's number), carrying the plasmid. Set the sensitive ones to dead.
  5. T2. 🫘 Harvest each founded T1 plant (its own line now): a T2 lot of, e.g., 35S:GFP-4.
  6. Test the T2. Sow some T2 seeds on selection, count resistant and sensitive seedlings, and on the T2 lot 🧮 Segregation test, expecting 3:1. Consistent with 3:1 means one insertion: keep that line. 15:1 means two.
  7. T3. Sow the chosen T2 lot, number the plants, and harvest each T2 plant separately: each gives its own T3 lot.
  8. Find the homozygous T3. Test each T3 lot expecting all resistant (1:0). Consistent with homozygous: on that lot ✎ Edit, zygosity homozygous. That lot is your stock: label it and record where it is kept.

Count enough seedlings: under about 30, the test cannot tell ratios apart reliably and says so.

A cross

  1. Both parents are plants in the seed bank (sown from their lots).
  2. Open the mother plant (the one that carries the seeds) and ✕ Cross with…, then choose the father (the pollen donor).
  3. This makes a new line (kind cross, parents the two lines, carrying both lines' plasmids) and its F1 seed lot, harvested from the mother. Name the cross if you like.
  4. F2: sow the F1 lot and 🫘 Harvest an F1 plant: the F2 lot of the same cross line. Genotype F2 plants (record it as a phenotype, trait genotype (PCR), or set their zygosity) to choose which to keep.

Phenotypes, photos and 3D scans

  • Record what you see on the plant (or on a lot, e.g. germination, or a line): ＋ Observation, with the date, a trait from the suggestions, and a value with its unit (day 24, 4.2 cm, pale).
  • Add the photos with the observation they show, so each photo has a date and a trait.
  • 3D scans (STL, PLY, OBJ, glTF) and other files (spreadsheets, PDFs) go under Photos, 3D scans and files on the plant; drag them onto it. 🧊 View in 3D shows a scan you can turn.
  • The same trait words every time make phenotypes comparable: flowering time, not sometimes bolting and sometimes flowered.

Storage and stock

  • Kept in: always fill it in, the same way, from big to small: Freezer 2 / box 4 / A1.
  • Amount: with its unit (300 mg, ~2000 seeds). Update it when you take a lot of seeds.
  • Used up: tick it when the bag is empty; the lot stays for its lineage, greyed in the list.
  • Germination and zygosity on the lot, once known.
  • Search the Seed lots list by location to see what is in a box.

Plasmids and the lab notebook

  • A line's plasmids come from the Plasmid Editor's library (Change plasmids…). Lines founded from a plant, and crosses, take their parents' plasmids automatically.
  • In the Lab Notebook, type @ in a page (or 🌱 Seed… in the Add bar) and choose the lot, plant or line you worked with. The record then lists that notebook page under In the lab notebook (for those allowed to open it).

Special cases

I harvested several plants together into one bag (a bulk).
Harvest one of them with 🫘 Harvest and write the others in the lot's notes (bulk of P-0012 to P-0019); mark the others harvested. Only do this for plants of the same line and generation, and only when you will not need to tell them apart.
Seeds of an old line nobody entered.
Make the line, then ＋ Seed lot with the source old stock, box 7 and whatever is known in the notes. Its history starts there.
A plant from a lot of another lab.
Other labs' stocks are not visible. Ask for seeds, then enter them as seeds from outside, with the source.
A mutant crossed into a reporter line, then selfed for several generations.
✕ Cross once; every generation after that is a 🫘 Harvest of the plant you keep: F1, F2, F3… of the same cross line.
I'm not sure which line a plant really is (it was mislabelled).
Say so in the plant's notes and set its zygosity to unknown; don't harvest it into a stock until it is checked.

Fixing mistakes

  • ✎ Edit corrects any field. Every change is kept under History on the record, with who made it.
  • A wrong segregation test or observation: remove it (✕) and enter it again.
  • A record made by mistake, that nothing came from: 🗑 on it. Records others came from cannot be deleted; mark them discarded or used up instead.
  • Seeds in the wrong line: on the lot, ✎ Edit and choose the right Line. This is possible until plants are sown from the lot; after that, note it on the lot and ask your lab lead. Don't re-enter the seeds as a second lot.
