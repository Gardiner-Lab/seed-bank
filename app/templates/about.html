Seed Bank {{ version }}

The Gardiner Lab suite's record of plant lines, the seed lots they are kept as and the plants grown from them, with how each descends from the others.

  • Lines: wild types, mutants, transgenic events and crosses, with their parents and the plasmids they carry (from the Plasmid Editor).
  • Seed lots: where they are kept, how much is left, germination and zygosity, and segregation tests on selection plates.
  • Plants: sown from a lot and numbered; harvested into the next generation, crossed, or the founder of a new line; phenotypes with photos, 3D scans and files.
  • Labels with QR codes that open the record on a phone.

A lab's stocks are shared within the lab and not seen by other labs.
